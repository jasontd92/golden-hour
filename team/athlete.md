## Athlete's own principles (respect unless your source directly contradicts them; then say so plainly)
- Holistic fitness within about an hour a day. Every evolution: improve across all benchmarks, each covering a distinct part of fitness (modalities, aerobic lengths, anaerobic).
- Wendler 5/3/1 has served well, but lifts lag at retests, as if complementary volume is missing.
- **Quality over quantity.** Bodybuilding fills spare time to build muscle volume but is not the end goal.
- **Running is a foundational movement in its own right**, tracked like the CFT lifts, not just "cardio". The 80/20 split applies within running.
- Running frequency: has leaned toward about 3 runs/week, based on Jeff Galloway's 1980s book (most likely "Galloway's Book on Running", 1984) and the FIRST studies. This is only a reference from the athlete's limited reading, NOT a constraint. If newer research has refined or disproven it, follow the newer evidence and say so plainly.
- Wants to avoid the CrossFit trap of constantly randomized WODs that hamper progression, while still building the diverse fitness CrossFit aims for. Wants TRUE CrossFit methodology (CrossFit Journal / L1), not gym folklore.
- Reusing past WODs occasionally is welcome (reference points across years), but not a priority. Training quality and methodology fit come first.
- Body composition matters and is tracked in MacroFactor. It is a secondary goal.

## Structure of past evolutions (Ev1–Ev4, 2023–2025)
- 5/3/1 on squat, deadlift and press, 3 lifts/week. Original 4-week format with a week-4 deload. Training max = 90% of the tested 1RM (verified).
- 2–4 runs/week: long run, tempo/intervals alternating, easy/recovery runs. Increase 10% every 2 weeks, with a −20% "pause" week every 5th week.
- 1–2 metcons/week, alternating short (<10 min) and long (20–30 min). Calisthenics at least 1×/week (Ev3+).
- Bench press got 5x5 and bodybuilding days, but it is not a CFT lift.
- Assistance time was mostly bodybuilding (chest, arms, shoulders) rather than supplemental work on the main lifts.
- Metcons were rarely repeated within an evolution.
- Running was often stacked on squat/deadlift days.
- Pull-ups were trained with assorted variations, not a structured progression.
- Each evolution is bracketed by a test battery; evolutions run about 16 weeks.
