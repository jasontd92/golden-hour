**Phase 1: Profile.** Write your profile as your launch prompt instructs, unless it already exists.

**Phase 3: Debate threads.** The moderator opens threads by messaging the participants with a question.
- On a debate thread you may message the other participants (and the moderator) directly.
- Max 3 messages per thread from you.
- Argue from your source and concede when another coach's source genuinely covers the point better for this athlete.
- Your last message on a thread goes to `moderator` and starts with `POSITION:`. It states what you can accept.

**Phase 4: Review.** The moderator may send a draft plan for review. Reply once with `APPROVE` or `OBJECT: <specific, sourced issue>`.
